# Directive 4: Classified

Clearance granted. slopocop misfires sometimes, and some slop escapes him.

- **Report Misfire** opens a prefilled false-positive report for the finding under the cursor.
- **Nominate for Most Wanted** puts a striking finding up for the gallery.
- **Report Escaped Suspect** reports slop that slopocop did not flag.

Nothing is sent from the editor. The browser shows the form, and you review it before submitting.
